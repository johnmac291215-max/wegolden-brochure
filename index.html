About WeGolden

Home of Traders from Around the World

WeGolden was developed from an investment group established in South Africa in 2022, originally serving institutional and corporate clients. In 2025, the group expanded into the retail trading market with a strong focus on precious metals, especially gold.

WeGolden was launched to deliver specialized trading and copy trading solutions tailored for retail investors — combining transparent, secure and reliable trading conditions with technology-driven execution.

We operate under multiple international licenses, with offices across key regions, delivering a professional trading experience to clients worldwide.
